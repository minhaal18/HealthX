About HealthX

Making healthcare easier to discover, understand, and access. HealthX brings hospital discovery, doctor information, surgery cost comparison, and digital health records together in one convenient platform.

Who We Are

HealthX is a healthcare platform concept designed to help people make more informed healthcare decisions. Our goal is to make essential healthcare information easier to find and help users keep their medical history organised.

Our Mission, Vision & Goal

Our Mission

Make healthcare information easier for everyone to access and understand.

Our Vision

Create a more connected healthcare experience for patients and healthcare providers.

Our Goal

Help users discover hospitals, compare available cost information, and organise medical records.

What HealthX Offers

Hospital Discovery

Explore hospitals and available healthcare information in one place.

Doctor Search

Find doctor information and specialisations.

Surgery Cost Comparison

Compare available procedure cost information to support informed decisions.

Digital Health Records

Keep important medical reports and health history organised.

Our Team & Hospital Research

Our team visited hospitals and interacted with doctors and patients to understand real-world healthcare challenges. We listened to their experiences, gathered valuable insights, and reviewed the difficulties people face while accessing healthcare services. This groundwork helped us identify opportunities for improvement and shape HealthX into a concept focused on making healthcare information more accessible, organised, and convenient.
